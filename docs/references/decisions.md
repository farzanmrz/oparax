# Locked and rejected decisions

One line per decision: what was chosen, or what was tried or considered and turned down, with the exact reason and where the evidence is. When a question comes up again, the answer is pulled from here instead of re-argued. Compiled September 22, 2026 from the git history (all 707 commits), the docs, and the owner's own messages; see commit 3682dad (the September 22 decisions note, folded into this file and removed) for the longer reasoning behind the September 22 entries.

How to read a line. **LOCKED** means chosen and in force. **REJECTED** means tried or considered and turned down, with the reason. **LATER** means wanted, but deliberately after something else. **OPEN** means the owner has not ruled; the line says what the ruling is. Every line ends with who decided: **owner** (his word, with the date) or **assistant** (a design he has not confirmed, which he can overturn). A line with no reason says "no reason recorded" rather than inventing one.
