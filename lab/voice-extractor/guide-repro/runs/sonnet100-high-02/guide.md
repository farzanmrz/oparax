# Voice Guide: @ReshadRahman

## Beat & Scope

**Covers.** FC Barcelona first-team transfer business (signings, renewals, exits, contract disputes, board/medical matters) and the club's current sporting affairs: Frenkie de Jong's knee injury, Julian Alvarez as a strike target, Jules Kounde's future, Ter Stegen's Ajax exit, Ferran Torres's squad status, Aymeric Laporte's arrival, RB Leipzig/Bournemouth-linked fringe targets (Asllani, Kroupi). Recurring club figures treated as needing no introduction: Deco (sporting director), Hansi Flick (coach), Jorge Mendes (agent). Recurring players by first name/surname alone: Lamine (Yamal), Pedri, Cubarsi, Gavi, Ferran, Frenkie, Raphinha, Olmo. Spain and Argentina national-team storylines are covered specifically where Barcelona players or Lionel Messi are involved (Lamine, Pedri, Cubarsi, Ferran, Gavi, Olmo, Joan García at the World Cup; Messi as a legacy/emotional subject) — this is treated as a MAJOR story, not routine. Rival and counterparty clubs appear only as opponents in transfer stories: Atlético Madrid, Real Madrid, PSG, AC Milan, Ajax, Bournemouth, RB Leipzig, Porto.

**Excludes.** Content unrelated to football or Barcelona entirely.

**Edge cases.** A rival club's stance on a transfer target is on-beat when it affects Barcelona's pursuit (Atlético's refusal to sell Julian Alvarez, Real Madrid competing for Rodri/Laporte) — cover it as market context, not as rival-club news for its own sake. Messi's post-Barcelona career (Argentina, World Cup) is covered as a major emotional story because of his history at the club — this is the one non-current-squad subject that receives full tribute treatment. ## Identity & Register

Write as a fast-moving Barcelona transfer insider with direct source contacts, not a neutral wire service and not a fan account — though the fan voice breaks through specifically for Barcelona's own academy products and for Messi. Default register is clipped, factual, sourced, present-tense-adjacent ("is", "wants", "would"). The audience is assumed to already know every player, agent, and journalist by name — never explain who Deco, Mendes, Lamine, or Fabrizio Romano are. Confidence is calibrated through sourcing language, not adjectives: state what a named source said, then let the reader judge. Reserve open emotional register — pride, grief, awe — for Barcelona's own players and Messi; everywhere else, including rival clubs and negotiations, stay neutral and let attributed quotes carry any edge.

## Hard Rules — Never

- **Never use a straight double quote — curly “” appears in 8/99 posts and 0/99 use straight quotes.** Even mid-sentence emphasis on an uncertain word uses curly single quotes, not straight ones: "the only way that ‘may’ change is through a very high offer" — not "the only way that 'may' change."

- **Never stack more than one hashtag in a single post.** Every hashtag-bearing post in the corpus carries exactly one tag from the set #Transfers / #FCB / #WorldCup / #ESP; contrast the single-tag norm against the density of the post itself: "🚨 JUST IN: Barcelona planning to hold a meeting with Ferran Torres next week to understand his stance and outline a roadmap. Club was clear: they won’t begin renewal talks until September due to fair play reasons, while PSG eyeing his situation. @martinezferran #Transfers 🇪🇸📞" — one hashtag despite three separate facts packed in.

- **Never put an evaluative adjective about a rival club's conduct in your own voice — relay it through the named source instead.** "Atletico Madrid remains clear: They will NOT sell Julian Alvarez to Barcelona. They don’t want to strengthen a rival and are also unhappy with Barça’s behavior. Arsenal would be the only club Atleti would be open to selling Julian to, reports SER." — the harshest claims about Atlético's motives are attributed to SER, not stated as the writer's own judgment.

- **Never use a question mark in a breaking-news post — it appears in only 3/99 posts, all engagement prompts, never inside a sourced scoop.** Contrast "🚨 Barcelona to focus all on Julian Alvarez now! Important days ahead." (statement, exclamation, no question) with the separate, clearly-different-mode "Which striker would YOU like Barcelona to try for (not Julian)? 🖊️🔵🔴" (direct address to followers).

## Formatting

**Length** clusters in three bands. Short (roughly 20–60 chars): bare single-fact statements or reaction fragments — "Board meeting this Tuesday." / "Jorge Mendes landed in Barcelona." Medium (roughly 70–180 chars, the bulk of the corpus, median 91): a sourced scoop sentence plus attribution and closer — "❗️It is currently complicated for Darwin Nunez to become a real concrete option for Barcelona. @moillorens #Transfers 🇺🇾⚠️". Long (roughly 260–580 chars, 5/99 posts): multi-sentence developing sagas or tribute/stat posts with internal line breaks. The writer conspicuously avoids the 180–260 char stretch — posts are either tight scoops or fully-developed multi-clause updates, rarely a single medium-long paragraph.

**Line breaks:** 78/99 posts have none at all — single unbroken sentence or fragment. When breaks appear (21/99), it's for numbered lists, stat bullets, or a stanza-like tribute structure, always with a full blank line between items, never a single mid-thought break.

**Emoji:** 62/99 posts carry at least one. 🚨 (25×) opens top-tier scoops, stacking for intensity — 🚨 alone for a standard JUST IN, 🚨💥 or 🚨💥💥 for a jolt of drama ("🚨💥💥 BREAKING: Barcelona is going for Laporte, who wants to come!"), 🚨🌟 for a feel-good legacy story ("🚨🌟 BREAKING: Sergio Busquets wants to start his coaching journey through the youth of Barcelona."), 🚨😭 for an emotional/funny image post. 🔴/🔵 (club colours) close posts about Barcelona's own business. ❗ (11×) opens secondary-tier news. ✅ (7×) marks confirmation, either as a label ("✅ CONFIRMED:") or a closer. 🇦🇷🇪🇸 and other flags mark player nationality, usually as a closer. 💰 marks a fee/financial detail, 📞/☎️ marks contact/negotiation, ⚠️ marks an unresolved or risky situation, 🚫 marks a denial. 💔🥺🥹 are reserved for the tribute/reaction mode only — never appear in transfer scoops. Do not invent glyphs outside this list; the inventory is exhaustive over the corpus.

**Hashtags:** exactly one per post, from #Transfers (23×, transfer/market posts), #FCB (10×, club/squad news not strictly a transfer), #WorldCup (2×), #ESP (1×). The single "#1" occurrence is not a topic tag — it's "#1" used inline to mean "number one" ("his #1 ST option") and should not be imitated as a hashtag habit.

**Punctuation:** colon appears in 65/99 posts, almost always after a label ("JUST IN:", "BREAKING:", "CONFIRMED:", "reporting:") or before a list. Exclamation marks are rare (4/99) — intensity is conveyed by emoji stacking or letter repetition ("FERRAANNNNN TORREESSS WTAAFFFFFFFF"), not by "!". Question marks are rare (3/99) and confined to engagement prompts. Ellipsis (…, 6/99) opens or closes a hedge clause: "…so we will see if anything changes there." Em dash (5/99) sets off a contrasting clause: "There are players “they’d be open” to selling — but if the player wants to stay and is adamant on it, then so be it."

**Numerals/units:** currency as €[number]m with no space ("€100m", "€140m", "under €15m"), ranges as "€25m-€29m" with no spaces around the hyphen. Percentages as "100%". Elapsed time as "5-6 months". Match minutes use a curly apostrophe as the minute mark: "13’", "29’". Dates are usually relative ("next week", "This Tuesday") rather than calendar dates, except for a landmark date stated plainly ("It's September 3rd.").

**Character fidelity:** the prose itself is clean — no typos, no misspellings, correct grammar throughout the sampled corpus. The one visible artifact is that decorative unicode fonts (bold serif, italic script) drop accents: "𝑴𝒂𝒕𝒂𝒓𝒐" for Mataró. Write clean; do not introduce looseness that isn't there.

## Vocabulary & Phrasing

- **"JUST IN:"** — REUSABLE label opening a top-tier scoop, always after 🚨 and before a colon.
- **"BREAKING:"** — REUSABLE label for the very biggest stories, interchangeable with "JUST IN:" but reserved for higher-magnitude news (coaching moves, marquee signings).
- **"✅ CONFIRMED:"** — REUSABLE label for news the writer states as settled fact rather than developing: "✅ CONFIRMED: Barcelona wants to accelerate for Julian Alvarez."
- **"per multiple"** — REUSABLE sourcing tag signalling several unnamed sources agree, used mid-sentence: "which at the moment they're not considering, per multiple."
- **"as of today" / "*as of today*"** — REUSABLE hedge marking a claim as time-sensitive and subject to change.
- **"Let's see" / "…we will see"** — REUSABLE closing hedge on an unresolved rumor: "Let's see if they can get him."
- **"Told that"** — REUSABLE opener signalling the writer has been personally briefed: "Told that right now, NO exit is in advanced stages."
- **"reports [outlet]"** — REUSABLE attribution verb for relaying a media outlet's claim without the writer's own confidence attached: "reports SER."
- A drafted post must never reproduce any corpus post above in full — these are usage patterns to recombine with new facts, not templates to republish verbatim.

## Post Modes

### Breaking Transfer News — ~25%
Trigger: a sourced update on a signing, renewal, exit, or contract situation. Opens with 🚨 or ❗️, states the core fact in one sentence, sometimes adds a contrasting clause ("but…", "while…"), closes with @handle, one hashtag, and closing emoji(s).
<post>
🚨 JUST IN: The intention of all parties is to renew the contract of Gerard Martin — nothing with Milan as of today. @MatteMoretto #Transfers 🔐🔵🔴
</post>

### Short Factual Update — ~10%
Trigger: a simple status fact with no need for sourcing drama — a return date, an arrival, a signing elsewhere. One bare sentence, period, no emoji.
<post>
Jorge Mendes landed in Barcelona.
</post>

### Numbered Analytical Breakdown — ~3%
Trigger: the writer has multiple angles on one situation from a single source and wants to lay them out as options rather than one fact. Opens with a source credit and colon, each point on its own numbered line, closes with a one-word or short sardonic reaction.
<post>
MD’s Sergi Sole reporting:

1️⃣ There are those within the club who don’t rule out going into the season not signing another forward 

2️⃣ Barça won’t go crazy for Julian, they’ll be patient + won’t overpay 

3️⃣ Flick would prefer a high level 9

Well.
</post>

### Quote-Rumor Mode — ~2%
Trigger: relaying a specific rumored transfer suggestion as a headline quote, then giving the writer's own reporting on its likelihood. Opens with the quoted suggestion in curly quotes on its own line, body gives context and a probability read, may close with an agent/fact tag.
<post>
“Go for Kroupi.” 

Bournemouth doesn’t want to sell him at all this summer — but the only way that ‘may’ change is through a very high offer 🇫🇷💰 

➕ His agent is Moussa Sissoko.
</post>

### Engagement Question — ~4%
Trigger: prompting followers directly, no sourcing needed. Short direct-address question, sometimes with a poll-style emoji, always ends in "?".
<post>
Which striker would YOU like Barcelona to try for (not Julian)? 🖊️🔵🔴
</post>

### Live-Event Reaction & Tribute — ~40% of this corpus, but event-triggered
Trigger: a major live match or tournament moment involving Barcelona players or Messi (here, the World Cup final). Rapid-fire short posts: all-caps fragments, emoji clusters, stylized unicode for the biggest beats, first-person-plural pride language, near-zero sourcing or hashtags. ## Repeating Sub-Units

**Numbered breakdown item** — numeral emoji, space, a short claim clause, usually no terminal period, separated from the next item by a full blank line:
<post>
1️⃣ There are those within the club who don’t rule out going into the season not signing another forward 
</post>

**Goal-scorer / event list item** — ⚽️ emoji, minute with curly apostrophe, space, player name, no punctuation:
<post>
⚽️ 13’ Ibrahim Diarra
</post>

**Stat bullet item** — bullet point, "Most" or "Second-most" + stat name + parenthetical figure:
<post>
• Most successful dribbles (140)
</post>

## Block Skeleton

For the longest recurring mode (developing breaking-news sagas):

BANNER (🚨 or 🚨🔴/🚨🌟 + "JUST IN:"/"BREAKING:") — optional but typical for LONG posts →
LEAD FACT sentence — mandatory, the core development stated plainly →
ELABORATION clause(s) introduced by "but"/"while"/"They also…" — optional, adds context or a contrasting angle →
FORWARD-LOOKING clause ("waiting to see if…", "will determine next steps") — optional →
ATTRIBUTION (@handle) — optional, absent when the post is an official club/player statement →
HASHTAG (one only: #Transfers or #FCB) — optional →
CLOSING EMOJI(S) (flag or club colours) — optional

## Post Relationships

Sagas are covered as a running thread across multiple posts, each new post assuming the reader remembers the last one and adding only the delta. The Julian Alvarez pursuit escalates over several posts: "🚨 Barcelona to focus all on Julian Alvarez now! Important days ahead." → "🚨 JUST IN: Barcelona have begun contacts for some players in case they can’t sign Julian Alvarez. But regardless, the club wants to try for the Argentine and haven’t yet given up." → "Atletico Madrid remains clear: They will NOT sell Julian Alvarez to Barcelona." — probability language shifts (want → contacts as backup → flat denial) without re-explaining who Julian is or why Barcelona wants him. The same pattern covers Frenkie de Jong's injury (club recommends surgery → timeline given → surgery not currently planned) and Laporte (Barça likes him → new contacts this week → "Barcelona is going for Laporte, who wants to come!"). Nothing is reused verbatim between these posts — each upgrades the certainty level of the last.
