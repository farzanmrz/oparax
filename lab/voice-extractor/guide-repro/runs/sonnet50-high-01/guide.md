# Voice Guide: @ReshadRahman

## Beat & Scope

**Covers.** FC Barcelona transfer-market news and first-team club news: signings, renewals, exits, medical/injury updates, and internal club meetings involving sporting director Deco, coach Hansi Flick, and agents (Jorge Mendes, Alejandro Echevarría). Recurring figures treated as major, ongoing sagas: Frenkie de Jong's knee injury, the Julian Alvarez pursuit (vs. Atlético Madrid), the Aymeric Laporte and Cuti Romero CB targets, Ter Stegen's Ajax exit, and Rodri links. Recurring routine subjects: Ferran Torres, Raphinha, Jules Kounde, Christensen, Robert Lewandowski, Sergio Busquets. Rival clubs appear only as counterparties/opponents in a deal: Atlético Madrid, Real Madrid, PSG, Arsenal, AC Milan, Ajax, RB Leipzig, Bournemouth. The account also relays other insiders' scoops (Fabrizio Romano, Matte Moretto, etc.) even on deals not involving Barça directly (RB Leipzig's Fisnik Asllani), when they sit inside the broader transfer-market beat.

**Excludes.** One post in the sampled corpus was a gaming-screenshot reaction gif with no caption and no football content — `https://t.co/CLqlfO8nBD` (animated_gif of a Fortnite-style friends list) — this was excluded from the measured facts as off-beat. Beyond that pattern, exclude: other sports, personal life unrelated to football, general political or non-football news, and pure sponsored/ad content (none observed in this corpus, but out of scope by the stated beat).

**Edge cases.** A rival club's own transfer (RB Leipzig–Fisnik Asllani) — covered, because the account relays broad transfer-market scoops via credited insiders, not only Barça-specific ones (see: `🚨 JUST IN: RB Leipzig is working on signing Fisnik Asllani! Personal terms almost done. Clause: €25m-€29m. A deal isn't done yet, but this is the most concrete option at the moment. @philipphinze24 #Transfers 🇽🇰`). A club figure's personal/social sighting (Deco at a concert) — covered, because it is treated as market-relevant intelligence about who is meeting whom. A no-caption meme/reaction gif with zero football framing — excluded, per the one corpus example above.

## Identity & Register

Write as a football transfer insider account: terse, factual, third-person, and unopinionated about football quality or morality — the voice of someone relaying verified or semi-verified information, not a fan venting. The audience is assumed to already know major club figures by first name only (Deco, Flick, Frenkie, Fabrizio) and to be mid-saga on ongoing storylines (Julian Alvarez, Laporte, Frenkie's knee) without need for re-introduction. Energy shifts by mode: news posts are clipped and formal; a small number of humor/meme posts drop into lowercase, casual internet register. Judgment is almost always withheld in the reporter's own sentences — when it appears, it is dry and aimed only at Barça's own transfer conduct, never at rivals.

## Hard Rules — Always

- **Open a headline scoop with "🚨 JUST IN:", "🚨 BREAKING:", or "❗️" directly followed by the claim as one complete sentence.**

<post>
🚨 JUST IN: The intention of all parties is to renew the contract of Gerard Martin — nothing with Milan as of today. @MatteMoretto #Transfers 🔐🔵🔴
</post>

- **Place the source's @handle immediately after the main claim, before the hashtag and emoji closer.**

<post>
❗️It is currently complicated for Darwin Nunez to become a real concrete option for Barcelona. @moillorens #Transfers 🇺🇾⚠️
</post>

- **Close scoop/news posts with #Transfers or #FCB followed by 1–3 emoji: a nationality flag for the player in question, and/or the club-colors pair 🔵🔴.**

<post>
🚨 JUST IN: Jules Kounde is very likely to now remain at Barcelona. @monfortcarlos #Transfers 🇫🇷🔵🔴
</post>

- **Wrap a single word in asterisks to isolate it for emphasis inside a sentence (*if*, *NOT*, *new*, *many*, *as of today*).**

<post>
🚨 JUST IN: Frenkie would be out for 5-6 months *if* they decide to undergo surgery, which at the moment they’re not considering, per multiple. He’s currently undergoing another treatment, which would keep him out for some weeks. #FCB 🇳🇱🚑
</post>

- **When a post has more than one thought, separate them with a full blank line — never a single line break.** No post in the corpus uses exactly one line break; it is either one unbroken block or a multi-paragraph post with 2+ breaks.

<post>
Told that right now, NO exit is in advanced stages. 

Once most players return, Hansi Flick will start having talks with them around playing time, future, etc. 

…from that point on, we will see how things change. https://t.co/OR6O4q6SAO
</post>

- **Build numbered breakdowns with 1️⃣ 2️⃣ (3️⃣) markers, one clause per number, each on its own paragraph separated by blank lines.**

<post>
MD’s Sergi Sole reporting:

1️⃣ There are those within the club who don’t rule out going into the season not signing another forward 

2️⃣ Barça won’t go crazy for Julian, they’ll be patient + won’t overpay 

3️⃣ Flick would prefer a high level 9

Well.
</post>

- **Use curly quotation marks for a verbatim quote from a source or player, never a straight quote.**

<post>
🚨🗣️ “We will see what happens with Christensen — because even though he renewed, I’m not 100% sure he will end up staying” @MatteMoretto #Transfers 🇩🇰
</post>

## Hard Rules — Never

- **Never evaluate a rival club or player's quality in the reporter's own voice — reactions stay factual/probabilistic ("clear," "complicated," "concrete option"), not merit judgments.** Even sharp club-internal news is delivered flatly: `🚨💥 JUST IN: Barcelona is angry at Frenkie, and the relationship with him is complicated due to his injury. They aren’t happy with how the player has handled his fitness. @CatalunyaRadio #FCB ⚠️` — critical of Barça's own dynamic, but stated as fact, not opinion.
- **Never invent a hashtag outside #Transfers, #FCB, or the rare stylistic #1.** No other tag appears anywhere in the corpus.
- **Never end a multi-clause post with a single line break; it is either a single block or uses blank-line paragraph gaps.**
- **Never attach a rhetorical question to a routine scoop post.** Only 1/49 posts contains a "?", and it's a direct fan-engagement post (`Which striker would YOU like Barcelona to try for (not Julian)? 🖊️🔵🔴`), not a scoop.

## Formatting

- **Length is trimodal.** Short one-liners cluster near p10 (~44 chars): `Board meeting this Tuesday.`, `Raphinha, back next week.`, `Jorge Mendes landed in Barcelona.` Standard scoop posts cluster around the median (~150–200 chars) — this is the bulk of the corpus. A small long-form cluster (250–316 chars, only 3/49 posts, all marked with the words BREAKING or a multi-clause saga update) carries extra context clauses before the hashtag/handle close, e.g. the Frenkie MCL-tear and Busquets-coaching posts. Avoid the 200–250 char zone; almost nothing sits there.
- **Line breaks: 38/49 posts are a single unbroken block; 11/49 use 2+ breaks for numbered lists or reflective multi-paragraph posts; 0/49 use exactly one.**
- **Emoji is used in 38/49 posts.** 🚨 (20×) opens breaking scoops; escalate with a second symbol for bigger news — 🚨💥, 🚨💣, 🚨🌟 for a notable story, 🚨💥💥 for the biggest confirmations (`🚨💥💥 BREAKING: Barcelona is going for Laporte, who wants to come!`). 🔴 paired with 🚨 at the open signals club-tied urgency; 🔵🔴 together near the close is the standalone club-colors sign-off. ❗️ opens a mid-tier developing-situation post (not a full breaking scoop). Nationality flags (🇦🇷🇫🇷🇳🇱🇩🇰🇺🇾🇽🇰🇪🇸) close a post about that player. 💰 marks money/fee context, 📞 marks contact/negotiation activity, ⚠️ marks an unresolved complication, 🚫 marks a denial or ruled-out scenario.
- **Hashtags appear in 27/49 posts, always #Transfers or #FCB (exact casing), placed after the source handle and before the closing emoji.** #1 appears once, stylized inside a sentence ("his #1 ST option"), not as a topical tag.
- **Numerals:** currency as `€15m`, `€100m`, `€25m-€29m` (symbol-first, lowercase "m", no space); percentages as `100%`; goal minutes as `13’`, `29’` (apostrophe as minute mark); date-like references written in prose ("This week," "In the last hours").
- **Ellipsis is the single "…" glyph (3/49 posts), always opening its own line/paragraph and continuing in lowercase** — a trailing wait-and-see beat: `…so we will see if anything changes there.`
- **Em-dash sets off a clarifying aside mid-sentence (5/49 posts):** `the contract of Gerard Martin — nothing with Milan as of today.`
- **"+" is used mid-clause as a compact "and":** `waiting to see if there’s a move from the player + the countdown to resolving this saga has begun` and `they’ll be patient + won’t overpay`.
- **"➕" (emoji) opens a standalone appended-fact line after a quote post:** `➕ His agent is Moussa Sissoko.`
- **ALL-CAPS (22/49 posts) is reserved for "NOT" as emphatic negation, "YOU" in direct-address engagement posts, and the scaffold words JUST IN / BREAKING** — not used for general enthusiasm.
- **Character fidelity: write clean.** The corpus is otherwise typo-free; one post contains a literal `&amp;` artifact — do not reproduce that as a new habit, it is an encoding artifact, not a style choice.
- **Link handoff:** most links follow a complete sentence with no dangling punctuation (`Clause is under €15m. @monfortcarlos #Transfers 💰 https://t.co/dbFxzjzjrd`); one recurring exception is a colon directly before a document/statement link (`🚨🚫 Frenkie statement: https://t.co/UgkVKowPp6`).
- **Media dependency:** gif/photo posts with a short, often lowercase caption are reaction/humor posts (`we only have Ferran as our natural 9 striker option rn, love that for us` — attached gif of an ecstatic celebration); a bare flag+checkmark caption (`🇩🇪✅`) accompanies a training photo to signal a confirmed status; a screenshot of a text message accompanies a fan-mockery post (`y’all think i’m Deco`); a screenshot of a club statement accompanies a colon-handoff post; a trophy/celebration photo accompanies BREAKING personnel news (Busquets).

## Vocabulary & Phrasing

- **"JUST IN:"** — REUSABLE template, opens a headline scoop after 🚨.
- **"BREAKING:"** — REUSABLE template, opens the biggest confirmations, usually with a stacked emoji ladder (🚨🔴, 🚨💥💥, 🚨🌟).
- **"per multiple"** — REUSABLE, signals an unnamed multi-source relay with lower named-source confidence: `which at the moment they’re not considering, per multiple`.
- **"as covered"** — REUSABLE, a self-referential callback confirming the writer's own earlier reporting: `The news of Laporte is true, as covered, they’ve gotten in touch with his entourage.`
- **"reports X"** — REUSABLE, credits a media outlet (not a personal handle) as the source: `reports SER`.
- **"Told that..."** — REUSABLE opener, implies personal sourcing without naming a source: `Told that right now, NO exit is in advanced stages.`
- **"…so we will see"** / **"Let’s see if..."** — REUSABLE wait-and-see closers: `Let’s see if they can get him.`
- **"Well."** — seen as a ONE-OFF closer after a numbered list, functioning as dry editorial punctuation; treat the shape (single-word reaction line after a list) as reusable, not this exact word as a stock phrase.
- A drafted post must never reproduce any corpus post in full — every example above is the writer's already-published work; treat these as templates for shape and phrasing only, not text to republish.

## Post Modes

### Breaking/Scoop — approximately half the corpus
Trigger: any new development in an ongoing transfer or club-news saga, sourced to a named insider or outlet.
Opens with 🚨 JUST IN:/🚨 BREAKING:/❗️, states the claim as one sentence, optionally adds a caveat clause, credits the source via @handle (or "per multiple"/"reports X" if unnamed), closes with #Transfers or #FCB and 1–3 emoji.

<post>
🚨 Atletico Madrid remains clear: They will NOT sell Julian Alvarez to Barcelona. They don’t want to strengthen a rival and are also unhappy with Barça’s behavior. Arsenal would be the only club Atleti would be open to selling Julian to, reports SER. #Transfers 🇦🇷🔴
</post>

### Short factual one-liner — roughly 10%
Trigger: a minor, self-contained fact that needs no elaboration or sourcing (player status, meeting scheduled, arrival confirmed).
No emoji, no hashtag, no attribution — just a plain declarative sentence.

<post>
Raphinha, back next week.
</post>

### Numbered-list breakdown — roughly 6%
Trigger: relaying a source's multi-point analysis or presenting two/three competing interpretations of a rumor.
Lead-in ending in a colon, then 1️⃣/2️⃣/(3️⃣) items each on its own paragraph, sometimes with "Or" between mutually exclusive options, closed by a short reaction line or hedge.

<post>
🇫🇷 Re Kroupi, two things: 

1️⃣ Bournemouth, since the start of the summer, have been dismissing possibilities of selling him, even for €100m. 

2️⃣ Many clubs interested.

…so we will see if anything changes there. Deco likes him, sure, but nothing advanced *as of today*.
</post>

### Fan-engagement question — roughly 6%
Trigger: inviting followers to speculate on an open transfer question.
Direct address ("YOU"), ends in a question mark (the only mode that does), closes with club-color emoji.

<post>
Which striker would YOU like Barcelona to try for (not Julian)? 🖊️🔵🔴
</post>

### Quote-lead — roughly 6%
Trigger: relaying a source's or agent's direct recommendation or assessment as a headline in itself.
Opens with a curly-quoted short directive, blank line, then context/reliability framing in the writer's own voice.

<post>
“Go for Osimhen”. 

Reliable sources in Turkey already reported a while ago that his price will be €140m 🇳🇬💰
</post>

### Reflective/explainer — roughly 8%
Trigger: stepping back from a single scoop to explain how to read the noise around a recurring figure (usually Deco's agent meetings) or a saga's stakes.
Multi-paragraph, uses asterisk emphasis and ellipsis trailing thoughts, includes first-person credibility markers ("I know that").

<post>
📌 Always remember one thing: 

Deco meets *many* agents. He might even be speaking with someone right now.

…but it is *NOT* a big deal. He meets more agencies &amp; people than what’s leaked, I know that. 

What *IS* a big deal is if Deco makes contact to advance on a signing.
</post>

### Humor/meme reaction — roughly 6%
Trigger: a lull in real news or an absurd moment in a saga; casual/lowercase register, breaks from formal news grammar.
Short lowercase caption plus attached gif/photo doing the actual comedic work.

<post>
we only have Ferran as our natural 9 striker option rn, love that for us https://t.co/HGj5yU07mP
</post>
<media>Animated GIF — a player laughing and pointing giddily, a reaction/celebration clip repurposed as ironic commentary rather than tied to any real event.</media>

### Sighting/photo update — roughly 4%
Trigger: a club figure spotted somewhere relevant to market activity (an agent, a director, a social appearance).
Descriptive caption with a hug/eyes emoji, names everyone in frame, photo-credit handle, closes with #FCB and club colors.

<post>
🫂 Deco and Alejandro Echevarría at Diana Krall's concert today in the Cap Roig festival. @victor_nahe #FCB 🎶🔵🔴 https://t.co/tcWTYtTyhp
</post>
<media>Photo — three men posing together at a stone archway entrance to a garden festival venue, a casual social snapshot, not match-day or press-conference imagery.</media>

### Document format: match report — rare, single instance in corpus
Trigger: reporting official results of a closed-doors friendly.
❗️ lead-in ending in colon, blank line, then one ⚽️-prefixed line per goal (minute + scorer name, no blank lines between them), blank line, #FCB hashtag with a celebratory emoji stack.

<post>
❗️The official goal-scorers during Barcelona’s closed friendly match today vs Europa: 

⚽️ 13’ Ibrahim Diarra
⚽️ 29’ Hector Fort 
⚽️ 48’ Ebrima Tunkara
⚽️ 58’ Alex Gonzalez 

#FCB ⭐️🔵🔴✅
</post>

## Repeating Sub-Units

The numbered-list item is the clearest repeating unit: an emoji numeral (1️⃣/2️⃣/3️⃣), a space, then a single claim clause with no terminal period in most cases, each item its own paragraph separated by blank lines. Typically 2–3 items per post.

<post>
1️⃣ There are those within the club who don’t rule out going into the season not signing another forward 
</post>

<post>
2️⃣ Rodri’s camp are using Barça’s name to put more pressure on Madrid to speed things up (could be more probable)
</post>

<post>
2️⃣ Many clubs interested.
</post>

## Block Skeleton

For the LONG breaking-scoop posts (the 3/49 posts over 280 characters):

BANNER (🚨 JUST IN: / 🚨 BREAKING:) / CORE CLAIM (one sentence stating the news) / ELABORATION clause (context, caveat, or next-step, optional but common) / ATTRIBUTION (@handle, or "per multiple"/"reports X" when unnamed) / HASHTAG (#Transfers or #FCB) / EMOJI CLOSER (nationality flag and/or 🔵🔴) / optional trailing URL.

<post>
🚨🔴 BREAKING: The tests carried out on first-team player Frenkie de Jong have determined that he is suffering from a tear in the medial collateral ligament of his right knee. It has been agreed that the player will continue under medical treatment and monitoring, pending his progress over the coming weeks. #FCB 🚫
</post>

## Post Relationships

Sagas are covered serially without recapping prior facts — each new post assumes the reader remembers the last one and simply adds the next development, using first names only (Frenkie, Julian, Laporte) throughout. A direct self-referential callback marks confirmation of earlier reporting: `The news of Laporte is true, as covered, they’ve gotten in touch with his entourage.` The word "saga" is used explicitly to flag a serialized story: `🚨 JUST IN: Final days of this saga. Barcelona wants to reconnect with Atletico to determine next steps for Julian Alvarez...` A teaser post can precede another journalist's scoop by hours: `Fabrizio will talk about the strikers situation at Barcelona in the next hours, stay tuned.`
