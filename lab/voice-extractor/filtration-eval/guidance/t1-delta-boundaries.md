# Filtration Guidance: @ReshadRahman — FC Barcelona Beat

## Core in-scope coverage

Treat any story where **FC Barcelona (the club, its first team, its executives, or its contracted players) is a direct subject or direct party** as in-scope. This includes:

- **Transfer activity in either direction** — official confirmations, negotiations, valuations, and even rumors that don't pan out, as long as Barcelona is a named buyer, seller, or blocked party. This is the dominant content type in the corpus and should be read broadly: a story where a target is *not* signing for Barcelona, or where Barcelona is *losing out* to a rival, is still in-scope.
<post>❗️Barcelona is still waiting for Julian Alvarez, they’re in a position to wait. They’ve not informed Atletico or his agent about withdrawing their offer/interest. Nothing new from Atletico’s end. @FabrizioRomano #Transfers 🇦🇷🚨</post>
<post>🚨 Atletico Madrid remains clear: They will NOT sell Julian Alvarez to Barcelona. They don’t want to strengthen a rival and are also unhappy with Barça’s behavior. Arsenal would be the only club Atleti would be open to selling Julian to, reports SER. #Transfers 🇦🇷🔴</post>

- **Contract, fitness, and squad-status news** for first-team players (renewals, injuries, disputes, exit speculation), even when the news is unflattering to the club.
<post>❗️Barcelona is angry at Frenkie, and the relationship with him is complicated due to his injury. They aren’t happy with how the player has handled his fitness. @CatalunyaRadio #FCB ⚠️</post>

- **Executive/board and ownership matters** (president health, board meetings, sporting-director activity like Deco's agent meetings).
<post>🚨 JUST IN: President Joan Laporta was unable to travel to England this week, as he was suffering from arrhythmia. Thankfully, the doctors have treated it well and his heart is now beating normally again. Healthy recovery, Presi! #FCB 💙❤️‍🩹</post>

- **Youth/reserve results** when tied to the pathway to the first team (e.g., Barça Atlètic friendly scorers) — keep, but do not expand into general lower-tier football coverage unrelated to Barcelona's structure.

## National-team stories involving current Barcelona players

Include these **only when the Barcelona player is the frame of the story** — their personal performance, quotes, reactions, or how the tournament affects their club status. Exclude generic national-team tactics, lineup debates, or other countries' squad news that merely happens to include a Barcelona player in a large roster without singling them out.

In-scope example (personal reaction tied to being a Barcelona player):
<post>❗️Fermin tells @CatalunyaRadio: “At first, I couldn't watch the Spain matches. It was tough as I also wanted to be there and play, since I was in-form. But the win meant a lot to me too, this is my country, and those are my teammates, especially the Barcelona players” #FCB 🥲</post>

Also in-scope: standalone posts spotlighting a Barcelona player's individual World Cup moment/quote (e.g., Cubarsi's reaction, Lamine Yamal highlights), since the corpus treats these as core content, not incidental mentions.

## Barcelona legends/alumni at other clubs

These are **borderline and should be filtered narrowly**: include only if the story is explicitly framed around the person's Barcelona legacy or history (retrospectives, "Barcelona legend" labeling, career tributes referencing their time at the club). Do **not** include ordinary current-club or national-team news about these individuals if the story has no Barcelona framing.

In-scope (explicit Barcelona-legacy framing):
<post>One defeat will not take away a lifetime of greatness, he’s in no need to prove anyone wrong. Never forget the happiness he’s given us all, especially to Barcelona when he was here.</post>
<post>❗️The Italian Federation is trying to convince Barcelona legend Pep Guardiola to manage the Italy NT. @SkySport #FCB 🇮🇹🚨</post>

Out-of-scope example type (would be excluded if it appeared): a story about Messi's current tactical role for Inter Miami, or Guardiola's Manchester City lineup decisions, with no reference to Barcelona.

## Rival-club and third-party transfer news

Exclude general transfer news about other clubs **unless Barcelona is a stated competing party or the story directly determines a Barcelona outcome** (e.g., a rival winning a race for a Barcelona target, or a selling club's stance affecting Barcelona's pursuit). A same-market transfer with no stated Barcelona linkage is out-of-scope, even if the player was previously discussed in unrelated contexts.

In-scope (Barcelona is a stated competing party):
<post>❗️Barcelona would also like to try for Rodri alongside Madrid and PSG, who are thinking of making a move for him. But nonetheless, Real Madrid leading the race. @FabriceHawkins #Transfers 🔍</post>

Out-of-scope examples (no Barcelona linkage stated in the story itself):
<post>🚨 JUST IN: RB Leipzig is working on signing Fisnik Asllani! Personal terms almost done. Clause: €25m-€29m. A deal isn’t done yet, but this is the most concrete option at the moment. @philipphinze24 #Transfers 🇽🇰</post>
<post>Cardoso Varela signs for Porto.</post>

If a similar story arrives with no explicit Barcelona tie in the text or retrieved content, exclude it even if the player's name has appeared in Barcelona rumors elsewhere — do not assume unstated background context.

## Additional nuance

- Personal/social appearances by club figures (executives, agents) count as in-scope only when the figure's Barcelona role is the reason for relevance (e.g., a sporting director's meeting doubling as transfer context), not generic celebrity-adjacent content.
- Injury/medical detail, salary disputes, and dressing-room tension are all in-scope even when reputationally negative for the club — do not filter out unflattering internal stories.
- Do not treat "friendly banter" or supporter-facing questions as a signal of scope; focus on whether the underlying incoming story involves Barcelona as club/player/executive subject matter per the rules above.
