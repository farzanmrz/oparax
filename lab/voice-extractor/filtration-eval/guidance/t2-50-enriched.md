# Filtration Guidance for @ReshadRahman (FC Barcelona Beat)

## Core coverage
This is a **transfer-and-club-news beat for FC Barcelona's first team**, not general football or general Spanish football. Approve any incoming story where **FC Barcelona (the men's first team, its front office, or its contracted players/staff) is a direct subject** — not merely mentioned in passing. Concretely, that means:

- **Transfer activity involving Barcelona**: incoming or outgoing rumors, official bids, agent talks, medicals, "here we go"/confirmations, rejected offers, renewal negotiations, release-clause or salary disputes. This is the dominant recurring category in the corpus (Julián Álvarez saga, Kroupi, Ferran, Ter Stegen, Gerard Martin renewal, Rodri links, Salinas, Goretzka denial).
- **Player and staff quotes/statements** about their future, form, fitness, or feelings toward the club (e.g. Balde on his future, Christensen on renewal, Fermin on Spain duty, Marc Bernal on his role).
- **Injuries, fitness updates, and medical decisions** (e.g. Frenkie's recommended surgery, Balde's pubalgia denial, Raphinha's return timeline).
- **Club governance/board news** directly tied to Barcelona (president's health, board meetings, financial matters affecting squad building — e.g. Laporta's arrhythmia, "board meeting this Tuesday").
- **Match/result news involving Barcelona teams**, including friendlies and youth/reserve fixtures if the source names Barcelona scorers or squad (e.g. the closed friendly vs Europa goal-scorers).
- **Rival or third-club stories that materially bear on a Barcelona negotiation** (Bournemouth refusing to sell Kroupi, PSG considering Ferran, Real Madrid leading for Rodri while Barcelona also inquired) — include these because Barcelona's interest/leverage is the actual news, even if Barcelona is not the front-runner.

Treat rumor-stage and unconfirmed reports as in-scope; this beat runs heavily on speculative/developing transfer information, not just confirmed signings.

## Exclude
- **Content with no real Barcelona-specific news content**, even if it appears on a monitored feed — e.g. unrelated personal/lifestyle posts, meme reactions, or ambiguous images with no caption tying them to the club.

<post>
❗️Christensen: “Leaving? It's not a question for me. I trust in my abilities and I hope to show my best level. I’m so grateful to the club for renewing my contract and for giving me this new opportunity” #Transfers 🇩🇰💙❤️
</post>
This is in-scope (player + contract). By contrast, a stand-alone image quote about "pain is temporary" (Lance Armstrong) attached to a otherwise-empty post, or a gaming-stream GIF, carries no Barcelona news value and should be excluded even if it comes from the reporter's own feed — it's personal filler, not a story.

- **General football news where Barcelona is not the actual subject** (e.g. pure Real Madrid or PSG transfer news, La Liga standings, other clubs' managerial news) unless Barcelona's interest, rivalry, or effect on a specific negotiation is explicit.
- **Engagement-bait/opinion prompts with no news event** — e.g. audience polls like "Which midfielder should Barcelona get to replace FDJ?" or "which striker would YOU like Barcelona to try for" — these are commentary/engagement devices, not incoming stories, and should not be mistaken for reportable news on their own.

## Nuances and borderline cases
- **Spain national team content**: Only in-scope when it's framed through a Barcelona player's relationship to the club (loyalty, injury while on international duty, effect on Barcelona's season) — not general Spain squad/tournament coverage.

<post>
❗️Fermin tells @CatalunyaRadio: “At first, I couldn't watch the Spain matches. It was tough as I also wanted to be there and play, since I was in-form. But the win meant a lot to me too, this is my country, and those are my teammates, especially the Barcelona players” #FCB 🥲
</post>

- **Journalist-sourced/aggregated transfer intel** (Fabrizio Romano, Matteo Moretto, etc.) republished or paraphrased should be treated the same as originally-sourced news — this is normal practice for this beat, not a red flag.
- **Vague or teaser-style posts** ("okay, time for Plan C", "Which midfielder should Barcelona get to replace FDJ?", "no fcb transfer news for 2 days") are reporter commentary/banter tied to an ongoing saga already covered elsewhere — do not treat these as separate stories; only the underlying news event (e.g. the actual Kroupi or Rodri report) should be scored as coverage-worthy.
- **Non-transfer personal/social appearances of club figures** (e.g. Deco at a concert) are low-priority but acceptable filler if clearly Barcelona-tagged, since the corpus shows this reporter occasionally posts such items — do not exclude outright, but they are not core to the beat and can be deprioritized relative to transfer/match/injury news.
- **Board/administrative posts with minimal detail** (e.g. "Board meeting this Tuesday") should only be flagged as a story if the retrieved content clarifies what the meeting concerns; a bare scheduling note without context is marginal and should lean toward inclusion only if linked content confirms Barcelona-relevant substance.
