## Original composition

Search filters titles, facts and publishers; selecting a story changes the reader. Direct uses the existing authored single-report samples, and Clustered uses the existing three verified historical stories. No extra news or statistics were fabricated. Original reports appear once in the selected reader.

The shared X conversation is a local preview that follows the selected story. Its displayed title, fact, date and click target stay together. An optional story prop preserves the Europa Clipper default for other directions. The window chrome and historical toolbar render on the landing only, avoiding a repeated feed heading. Shared RoadmapList and Pricing appear once each, on the landing only. These compositions demonstrate the free components and are not installed React Bits Pro marketing or Application UI blocks. No paid source was accessed or copied.
