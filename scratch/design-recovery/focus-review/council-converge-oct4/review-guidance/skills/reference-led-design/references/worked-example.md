# Worked example: Oparax feed, October 2, 2026

How the accepted feeds were reached. Images are in `../examples/` (accepted/, near-misses/, rejected/).

## Before and after

- `rejected/rejected-paragraphs-in-boxes.png`: a column of prose cards beside an aside of sentences. Owner: "neither the sections their components nor the design hits. Nothing lands."
- `rejected/themes/`: the four researched palettes on one quick page, then the same four on the feed (graphite, slate, ink, navy, plus graphite light). Owner: "I despise all four of these... There's still no life on the pages."
- `accepted/` (Window, Newsroom and Deck, dark and light): the accepted round. Owner: "This is so beautiful... all three of them... I love all three of them in dark and light mode."

## The three directions (all accepted)

1. Window: an app window on a lit frame; sources with real favicons on the left; a story list with a live checking row; the open story large with each report as its own row; status tiles on the right.
2. Newsroom: a dashboard table, one row per report under a mono uppercase header, a live row on top, rows that expand in place to facts and quotes; status tiles and a small chart beside it.
3. Deck: a slim row of tiles on top; stories as cards, with clustered stories as physical stacks whose other reports peek behind them.

## Color roles that worked (dark values)

| Role | Value | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Page | `#0b0c0f` | quiet ground, faint blue tint |
| Window | `#111317` | the one lifted surface |
| Rail | `#0e1013` | navigation, a step below the window |
| Hairline | white 7.5 percent, 13 percent on controls | every edge |
| Text tiers | `#f3f5f8`, `#c9ced7`, `#8d939e`, `#646a75` | order of reading by brightness |
| Brand | `#6b95ff` (solid `#3a6cf4`) | primary action, selection, focus, X post mark |
| Article | `#3cc9b5` | article kind, teal so it never reads as healthy |
| OK | `#4cd07d` | live, healthy |
| Caution | `#f2b84b` | checking, warnings |
| Error | `#f2555a` | failed |
| Stage light | radial white-blue at 20 percent from the top | the soft light behind the window |
| Window shadow | layered black, 16 to 96px blur | lifts the window off the page |

Light mode: page `#eceef2`, white panels, borders black at 10 to 16 percent, brand `#2459e8`, article `#0d9488`.

Known issue from the council: the dimmest tier measured about 3.2:1, under the 4.5:1 body-text floor. Use it for decoration and small labels only, never body text (as DESIGN.md says).

## Process that produced it

Verbatim owner messages, a 130-image board with his verdicts beside our renders, ten criteria from his words, three council lanes each citing board images and converging on three compositions, one builder designing against the images with real data and self-checking its screenshots against the rejected set.
