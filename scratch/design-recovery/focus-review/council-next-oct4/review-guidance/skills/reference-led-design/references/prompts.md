# Prompt templates

Fill the angle brackets. Keep the owner's words verbatim. Give absolute paths.

## Council lane: propose directions (advice)

```
The owner's request (verbatim): "<latest message>". His messages since <date>, verbatim and in order: <path>.
What the host did and why it failed (the host's account, not data): <short, honest>. Agree or disagree explicitly.
What he said that the last renders ignored (every direction must meet all of them): <numbered criteria with quotes>.
The examples: open every image in <skill>/examples/accepted/, near-misses/ and rejected/, with the verdicts and component notes in SKILL.md sections 1 and 2.
Product truth: <path>. Components available: <catalog paths or the react-bits-pro, shadcn and ai-elements skills>.
Questions: 1. As a human looking at the accepted feeds beside our latest renders, what is the difference? 2. Is any locked spec of his part of the problem? Phrase changes as yes or no questions. 3. Who uses this screen and what are they there to do (SKILL.md section 2a)? 4. Propose <3> directions that differ in the structure of the whole screen, each at the accepted feeds' level of depth, color and imagery, with layout, objects and their real data, the catalog components behind them, the person's task on it, where color appears and what it means, light mode, and its nearest near miss and why it is not that. 5. Which first and why.
```

## Builder

```
You are the single builder. The bar: placed beside the three accepted feeds in <skill>/examples/accepted/, your screens must look like the same product made by the same hand, at the same level. If a screenshot could be mistaken for anything in examples/near-misses/ or examples/rejected/, you have failed; fix it before reporting.
Read SKILL.md first, then OPEN every example image. Then the agreed directions <path>, the owner's words <path> and the criteria <path>; every screen must meet every criterion.
Use: name the person and their task (SKILL.md section 2a); every element must help it. Walk the task on the built page (arrival, action, result, empty and error states, keyboard focus, a narrow window).
Content: real data only; never invent counts, deltas or activity; label any staged motion once.
Build: <routes>, composed from the catalog components first (custom styling only when it adapts one), in the accepted palette, every hue with one job, depth by light, real images, real light mode. Write only in <scope>.
Verify: typecheck and build pass. Screenshot every screen in dark and light at 1440x900 into <path>. Place each beside the accepted feeds and the near misses and iterate. Report in your reply (no new files): per screen, what it shows, which accepted-feed traits it carries, where each hue appears and what it means, what data is real versus preview.
```

## Council lane: review a render (critique)

```
Ask with equal weight: placed beside the three accepted feeds, does this belong with them at their level, or does it look like a near miss or rejection? Does it use the components imaginatively? And can the person do their task on it at a glance (SKILL.md section 2a)? Then check truth to the product and the criteria. Cite example filenames for every finding.
```
