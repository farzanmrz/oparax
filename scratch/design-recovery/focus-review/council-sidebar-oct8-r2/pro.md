I am withdrawing my initial Option 1 (The Global Rail) and revising my position to reach consensus, answering every idea from Astra, Grok, and the host.

**Header (a):** I withdraw my proposal for no header. I accept Grok and the host's thin 48px mark-only header inside the centred column, because the owner explicitly stated, "I think the page is looking blank because it doesn't have a header" (October 5). For this same reason, Astra's idea for no signed-in header must go. 

**The rail (b):** I accept Astra and the host's one continuous rail surface. Grok's three lifted plates must go because the owner noted "I don't know how cards would look on it once we have the entire width of the page" (October 3), and dividing the sidebar into distinct floating cards makes it look disjointed rather than like a unified workspace tool. I accept the host's approach to make the foot distinct via a hairline and `--raised` ground instead of a separate plate.

**Section headings (c):** I withdraw my uppercase mono captions without icons. I accept Astra, Grok, and the host's collapsible summaries (13.5px semibold, kind icon, count, chevron). The owner explicitly ruled to "make the X accounts, RSS feeds, and each section expandable and collapsible, with the icons for them" (October 3) and complained that "The number on the section header is not aligned, so it looks horrible." 

**Filter state (d):** I withdraw my clear icon inside the sidebar. I accept Astra, Grok, and the host's "from {name}" chip beside the page title, as it keeps the active filter context immediately visible in the content area even when the sidebar is completely closed.

**Sidebar top:** I accept Grok and the host's idea to have Feed, Settings, and Notifications as text links with the current page underlined in the brand color, as it clearly grounds the user's location. 

**Sidebar middle:** I accept Astra, Grok, and the host's grouping of sources with real logos and right-aligned counts, completely removing the redundant "All sources" button that the owner called useless.

**Sidebar foot:** I accept the host's pinned foot on a `--raised` ground with a hairline above, containing the free-week meter, theme toggle, initial/email, bordered Sign out, and Hide. This perfectly satisfies the owner's request that these bottom options "appear differently" from the sources. 

**Onboarding sidebar:** I accept Astra, Grok, and the host's plan to show the seven steps in the middle section until the agent is saved, replacing them with the sources once ready, while keeping the navigation and foot stable. 

**Collapse:** I accept Grok and the host's approach where Hide removes the whole rail and a "Sources" control beside the page title brings it back. Astra's bottom-left Menu button must go because tying the restoration to a control beside the page title better unifies the page actions and perfectly satisfies the rule "Just when the sidebar is closed, it's closed" without leaving orphan icons in the bottom corner.
