RESULT: FINDINGS.

I would pass S3 as a concept and replace S1 and S2 with the revisions below. These are design judgments, not verified usability results.

The daily reader follows a beat and publishes about it. They need to catch up, understand each story and trust its sources without opening articles.

[Window](/Users/farzanm4/.agents/skills/reference-led-design/examples/accepted/accepted-window-dark.png) gives reading, navigation and evidence distinct spaces within a visibly lifted surface. [Newsroom](/Users/farzanm4/.agents/skills/reference-led-design/examples/accepted/accepted-newsroom-light.png) makes dense information orderly, with convincing white surfaces and inset evidence. [Deck](/Users/farzanm4/.agents/skills/reference-led-design/examples/accepted/accepted-deck-dark.png) gets life from substantial images, varied silhouettes and source layers. Transfer their hierarchy, depth and functional color. Their older terminology, separate digest treatment and hidden facts are not requirements.

The [front-page near miss](/Users/farzanm4/.agents/skills/reference-led-design/examples/near-misses/feed-same-shell-front-page-dark.png) already has impressive imagery and readable facts. What it lacks is a different whole-screen proposition. [Convergence](/Users/farzanm4/.agents/skills/reference-led-design/examples/rejected/feed-convergence-lines-dark.png) spends space explaining machinery. [Paragraphs in boxes](/Users/farzanm4/.agents/skills/reference-led-design/examples/rejected/rejected-paragraphs-in-boxes.png) lacks object hierarchy and visual life.

S1 fails as written. Interlocking tiles can become Deck with administration inserted between stories. A large quotation must not duplicate a fact below it. Its front-page resemblance remains high.

S2 fails as written. Four narrow columns containing facts and evidence create competing reading tracks; sideways arrows make catching up unnecessarily awkward. Equal columns also weaken the leading object.

S3 passes conceptually. Continuous reading with adjacent evidence serves trust directly. Its dock changes the screen’s organization. It fails if sidenotes become connecting wires or every sheet becomes another identical text box.

Build these three, in this order, after both lanes clear them:

First, continuous annotated sheets, revised S3. Stories begin near the top; account, source filters and status occupy a bottom dock with reserved space. Each lifted sheet contains the headline, all cited facts and adjacent verbatim evidence. GPT-6.1 uses Simon’s image; imageless items begin directly with content. Unlike Window’s selected reader, Newsroom’s table or Deck’s masonry, the entire page is the reading sequence. The nearest miss is [Reader](/Users/farzanm4/.agents/skills/reference-led-design/examples/near-misses/feed-same-shell-reader-dark.png), but there is no thumbnail selector or selected-story dependency. This best supports reading and checking evidence.

Second, open folios, replacing S2. Two broad story pages share each lifted spread, followed vertically by further spreads. A compact source index occupies the opening corner; status occupies its outer margin. Pair Olmo and MAI first, retaining publication order and all facts. Images sit beside text; imageless pages use attributed evidence without artificial covers. This supports scanning adjacent stories. It differs from Deck through shared spreads and from Reader through multiple complete stories without selection.

Third, integrated board, revised S1. The beat, grouped source controls and status form one corner object within an asymmetric board; complete story tiles occupy the remainder. Tile dimensions follow content length, not image availability. Olmo leads; MAI and Vercel Agent support it. Unlike the front-page miss, navigation participates inside the composition, with no surrounding rail, masthead or title-toggle band. This supports rapid overview and filtering.

All three retain exact theme tokens: teal article citations, blue posts/actions, neutral GitHub marks, green live, amber checking/free week and red failure. Native imagery supplies broader color. Light mode uses white lifted surfaces, pale ground and visible ink borders.

Verified limitation: [feed.ts](/Users/farzanm4/Desktop/repos/oparax/scratch/design-recovery/site/next/data/feed.ts) gives every clustered story an image; imageless examples occur in Direct. GitHub lacks a recorded explanatory synthesis. Do not invent one or Product Hunt entries.

The strongest objection is that dock placement alone cannot rescue familiar centers. Highest risks are repetition, cramped evidence and inflated GitHub claims. Start with S3’s dark/light 1440×900 comparison; rendered quality and interaction remain unverified.
