I favor a continuous reading roll, with an open folio as the second candidate. Both are composition proposals, not cleared designs. Their visual finish and usability remain unverified.

The six accepted images establish transferable traits: visible lift, quiet illumination, functional color distributed across objects, recognizable publisher marks, substantial imagery, dense information with clear hierarchy, and consistent type and edges. Window succeeds through containment and a readable selected story; Newsroom through aligned scanning and expansion; Deck through independent surfaces, image rhythm and tangible report stacks. Their shared chrome is incidental to those successes.

The highest risks are repeating the shell in `feed-same-shell-front-page-dark.png`, letting photography displace facts as in `feed-same-shell-photo-lead-dark.png`, and making provenance into machinery as in `feed-convergence-lines-dark.png`. `rejected-paragraphs-in-boxes.png` and `rejected/themes/` show that orderly text and correct colors alone cannot carry the screen.
