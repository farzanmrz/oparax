The product, as it stands in my head
A person comes in, says the kind of news they want to monitor, and provides their X account. A Grok-based agent reads their presence on X and builds two things:

* A picture of what they want monitored.
* A picture of who they are.

It shows both back to them, then news arrives.
The gate we are basing everything on, carried over from `exp1.md`:

* Providing the handle is free.
* Connecting the account, or personalizing interests, beliefs, or sources, requires signup.

Dimensions to explore
1. What to monitor, read from X
My current thinking. The first dimension is what news the person wants. It comes from:

The gap to preserve. I could follow Ronaldo, The Rock, and Sam Altman and still only want AI news. Whatever we read from X has to be reconciled with what the person actually said they want.
2. The person: biases, interests, beliefs
My current thinking. This is the axis I have avoided and it is time to introduce it. What the person feels, thinks, and is biased toward. From it, the agent relates back to their posts: these are the articles they talk about, these are the websites they cite, so this is the kind of news to track. It then shows the person: this is the beat we are monitoring, based on what you told us and what I learned. What they see, and what they can correct or remove, is open.
Where it gets hard. Corrections come in two kinds, and I do not know how to separate them yet:

1. Source-level. "This source is not trusted" or "this is irrelevant." They override the source.
2. Topic-level. "I do not want news about the women's FC Barcelona team." The agent picked a story class they have no interest in. This is the murky one, because it is their beliefs and interests, not a source list, that has to change.

4. Relevance, urgency, and what the feed shows
The idea I blanked on. Whatever the feed or the DM shows should carry indicators of relevance, popularity, and recency. What has to exist now. If we start from the person's description of the beat, relate it to their actual activity on X, and from that target the X and internet sources to monitor, we will surface a lot of things that need some categorization and representation:

* This is more urgent.
* This is specifically from your sources.
* This has less activity around it.
* This is an opinion piece.
