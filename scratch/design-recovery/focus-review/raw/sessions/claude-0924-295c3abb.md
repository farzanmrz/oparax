## Mail reading and analysis
Spark is much more capable than just listing unread mail.
It can:
- **Search by metadata:** Sender, recipient, CC, subject, date, account, folder, read state, starred state, pinned state, unreplied state, attachment type, filename, category, and assignment state.
- **Search by meaning:** A topic search uses both keyword and semantic matching and returns relevant email bodies. For example, it can find conversations about “Railway costs” even if those exact words are not in every subject.
- **Search every folder:** It can search Inbox, Archive, Sent, custom folders, and both accounts together. Trash, Spam, and Blocked are excluded unless deliberately targeted.
- **Read full threads:** It can inspect every message in a conversation, including sender, recipient, timestamp, body, attachments, and the Spark deep link.
- **Inspect attachments:** It can identify attachments and read or stream a particular file when the content is actually needed.
Useful examples include:

```text
Show unread priority mail across both accounts.
Find every conversation with Sophie, including archived mail.
Show unreplied messages from people from the last seven days.
Find anything about Railway billing or a trial ending.
Read the full Drew Bury conversation.
Find emails containing invoices or spreadsheet attachments.
```
