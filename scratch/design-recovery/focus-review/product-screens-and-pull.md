## B. What pulls users in

- Target user (docs/roadmap.md section 1): "people who follow a beat and publish about it. AI content creators first (owner, August 27) ... reporters second", first cohort of five known people (Farzan, Kush, Liam, Nihan, Reshad); owner builds their agents and sends links.
- Value proposition (owner, roadmap section 1): "You already live on X, you already see X. What about the wide internet that you don't see on X? Oparax brings it to you." and "I'll monitor the internet, GitHub and Product Hunt for you outside of X and give you that info on X." One DM per story with a link (decisions.md).
- Discovery (docs/discovery/reshad.md, findings.md): Reshad "this is incredible", "75% there" (family, discounted); real use "to catch up on stuff and mainly see if I've not missed out on anything"; "the pain is not being first, it is completeness" (file's analysis); wanted auto-suggested sources; accuracy is the bar (a mistranslation he would not post); wants speed. Liam liked that Oparax "filters out unrelated stories instead of pulling everything from the sources"; GitHub "useful eventually". Nihan: setup "looked quite close to how he actually approaches content".
- Drop-off: reporters 178 listed, 89 reachable, 5 chatted, 5 of 5 get news by text or calls before X. Creators 23 listed, 11 chatted, 2 demos, 0 active; 5 of 11 raised money first; 4 of 11 went silent at the first ask requiring work; Liam and Nihan stalled after the demo. Reshad idle 10 days after daily founder contact stopped ("the strongest falsification in the record"). I: the product must deliver value with near-zero user effort.
- Aha: no stated metric. exp1.md defines "meaningful-use day" and "repeated benefit". Candidate aha (I): first populated feed from one's own beat plus the first DM.
- Pricing: Hobby $5 (100 watched X posts a month, daily), Creator $30 (3,000, daily), Wire $99 (4,000, every 15 minutes when news). Sites and feeds unlimited. Free week: 7 days, 300 posts, no card. Margins about 50%, 37%, 30% (52% normal week). Reshad's stated willingness to pay: $4 a month.

## C. Constraints a design must respect

- Card contract (lib/feed/types.ts): headline (one line), facts 1 to 5, each with 1 to 3 evidence quotes `{item, span}` (per-fact source links and verbatim quotes available), publishers `{source_id, name, url}`, image nullable, headline_from. Story also has reports[] with url, title, published_at, kind article|post, author, publisher name.
- Images: optional. Article image from feed enclosure, media thumbnail, first body image or og:image. X post image: first photo only. Card image = first attached item's image. Not available: publisher favicons or logos, post author avatars (would need deriving), relative time (only ISO timestamps).
- DM (lib/alerts/pack.ts): plain text "Oparax: N new story/stories for you", then per story headline, first fact, link `https://oparax.ai/{handle}/{story id}`. Max 10 stories. No image, quote or buttons. Wire every 15 minutes only when news; Hobby/Creator once a day at the owner's hour.
- Plan and state: free week 300 posts; pool stops at zero; day-7 freeze keeps stories readable; settings editing paid-only; one agent per account; up to 10 sites and at least 5 X accounts recommended; beat max 300 chars.
- Design contract: DESIGN.md (navy/blue, dark default plus equal light mode, Open Sans target, 44px touch targets under 700px, footer only Privacy/Terms/Contact, logo links home). Onboarding progress must reflect real log entries, not invented reasoning. Roadmap must not imply planned platforms work.
