I reject proposing identical stacks of chips for Onboarding step 4 or a blanket component with divisions because: "It's all just one consistent thing instead of having some imaginative UI that is also coherent." I reject using a workspace switcher or nested tree for the sidebar because: "Those are differently categorized parts of the sidebar, whereas sign out, free week, hide sidebar, and all of that are separate." I reject using a single large attached card for Settings because: "Why are these attached cards? Why are they not separate cards?"
