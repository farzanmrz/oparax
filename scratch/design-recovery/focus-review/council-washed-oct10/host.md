# Host (Claude Fable 5.1), written before reading the lanes

1. Diagnosis. In light the One stacks four near-whites with steps of 2 to 5 percent: page #eceef2 (lightened further at the top by the 11 percent blue stage light), rail #f7f8fa, band #f4f5f8, card and window #ffffff. The accepted light feeds separate by composition, not by hue: Window puts its white rail inside a lifted window on a darker lit frame (#dfe3ea), Deck's white tiles and cards sit on the plain #eceef2 page with a visible ring and a shadow that reads, Newsroom draws table lines at 10 to 16 percent. The One lost two of those: the rail is a near-white panel against a near-white page with one 10 percent line between them, and the band on a card is a 4 percent step off white, so a card's header and its body read as one surface. The stage light makes the top of the page, where the first cards sit, paler than the page below, so the cards near the title have the least contrast. Dark does not suffer because its steps are 3 to 6 percent of a dark range, which the eye reads as bigger, and the shadows are black at 0.6.

2. Directions.
A, Deeper ground: light page #e4e7ed, stage light cut to 5 percent and only behind the title line; rail #f9fafb with a 14 percent right line; card #ffffff with a ring at 18 percent and the card shadow at 0.14 to 0.28; band #eef0f4 (a 7 percent step off white) with a 10 percent line under it; well #eceef2; lines 14, 8, 20 percent. Dark moves the same way: page #0b0c0f, rail #0e1013, card #14171c, band #1e222a, well #0a0b0e, lines 10, 6, 16 percent, shadows as they are. Changes: the sidebar reads as a panel on a darker page, the band reads as a header, every card edge reads. Risk: a darker page is a step toward "pale-washed dark" only if it goes grey; #e4e7ed stays cool and light.
B, Lit frame: keep the page #eceef2 but draw the One's column on Window's lit stage frame (#dfe3ea to #eceef2) behind the cards, rail as in A, cards with a 16 percent ring and 0.12 to 0.24 shadow, band #eef0f4. Dark: the frame gradient #1a1d24 to #0d0e11 behind the column. Changes: the whole feed sits in a lit field, the cards lift off it. Risk: a gradient behind cards is near the "gradient wash" near miss; it is Window's own accepted device, so it passes if it stays as subtle as Window's.
C, Lines only: grounds unchanged; lines 16, 10, 22 percent everywhere, a 1px line under every band, a 14 percent right line on the rail, card ring 20 percent, no shadow change. Dark: lines 11, 6, 18 percent. Changes: every edge reads; the surfaces still sit on a near-white page. Risk: lines without depth are Newsroom's device, not Deck's; the cards may read as boxes.

## Directions
A, Deeper ground (page darker, rail lighter, band a 7 percent step, rings 18 percent, stage light cut).
B, Lit frame (Window's stage frame behind the column, rings 16 percent).
C, Lines only (every edge at 16 to 22 percent, grounds unchanged).
